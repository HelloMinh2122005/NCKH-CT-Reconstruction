Second-Order Variational Energy Minimization

At each stage \(t\), the image update minimizes a local quadratic energy surrogate combining physical projection fidelity, proximal anchoring, and the learned anatomy prior:

$$\min_{x} \left[ \frac{\lambda_t}{2} \|Ax - y\|_2^2 + \frac{\mu_t}{2} \|x - x_t\|_2^2 + \langle x, \nabla\mathcal{R}_\theta(x_t) \rangle \right]$$

Setting the Fréchet gradient to zero yields the standard linear system solved via matrix-free Krylov conjugate gradient without ever materializing the massive \(65,536 \times 65,536\) Hessian matrix:

$$(\lambda_t A^T A + \mu_t I) x_{t+1} = \lambda_t A^T y + \mu_t x_t - \nabla\mathcal{R}_\theta(x_t)$$

Windowed Self-Attention & Relative Position Bias

To prevent missing-wedge streak artifacts from leaking globally across healthy organ tissues, self-attention is strictly confined to non-overlapping \(8 \times 8\) spatial patches:

$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{Softmax}\left( \frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d}} + B_{\text{rel}} \right) V$$

The 2D Relative Position Bias \(B_{\text{rel}} \in \mathbb{R}^{(2M-1)^2 \times N_{\text{heads}}}\) preserves true vertical spatial continuity parallel to X-ray rays, resolving the structural disruption caused by 1D raster scan flattening in sequence models.
